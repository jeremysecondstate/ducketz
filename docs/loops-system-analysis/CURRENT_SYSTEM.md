## Preparation and feedback

The locally inspected replacement for Loops Overnight Gameplan (LOG) is a
Stats-first, dependency-driven workflow. LOG's native definition remains paused.
Its numerical stages remain useful; its old all-in-one recurrence is historical.

1. Catch up the required completed-session data, retaining provider identity,
   coverage receipts, availability clocks and separate operational/archive data.
2. Score matured original forecasts against actual outcomes, retain pending and
   unavailable results, and freeze verified Stats before model review.
3. Review the saved Stats with a bounded structured model proposal. Numerical
   development selection, calibration, held-out assessment and promotion decide
   acceptance; a proposal is not an approved model or a source-code edit.
4. Prepare each producer's local immutable Gameplan, enrichment and planning
   inputs. Preserve original forecast identity, universe, target windows and
   model assessments. An explicit no-history baseline invents no scores.
5. Exchange verified private owner packages. Scout synthesizes the combined
   capital plan and combined Stats; Atlas receives and verifies the frozen
   result. Atlas does not independently recompute Scout's synthesis.
6. Verify the dated local and combined publications, their exact source bindings
   and default readers. Local completion, joint readiness and trader readiness
   are separate results; a launched task is none of these by itself.

The relevant inspected modules are `ml/nightly_dispatch.py`,
`ml/nightly_workflow.py`, `ml/nightly_readiness.py`, `ml/nightly_recovery.py`,
`ml/gameplan_model_feedback.py`, `ml/gameplan_stats_handoff.py`,
`ml/nightly_synthesis.py`, `ml/nightly_handoff.py` and
`ml/nightly_joint_readiness.py`. Several are local working-tree additions absent
from the publication base; per-file installation evidence is separate, and [exact pending source references](RELEVANCE_REVIEW.md#evidence-and-revisions)
are essential. Their presence in the working tree is not proof that a running
process loaded those bytes or that the peer installed them.

Normal publication retains its pre-opening deadline. Explicit reviewed late
recovery is a separate, bounded path: it preserves the missed original deadline,
actual publication time, frozen recovery deadline and pre-action input cutoff.
It must not backdate forecasts or present a late plan as an ordinary on-time run.
Resume keeps completed outputs and the run's recorded stage list; older runs do
not acquire new stages merely because the current source has changed.

## Readers, capital and execution

The Gameplan and Stats readers are separate artifact consumers. Gameplan prefers
an accepted verified combined plan when eligible, while retained historical
publications remain readable. Stats combines source-bound evaluated counts and
outcomes; it does not average percentages or treat missing outcomes as zero.
A combined display, a prior reader verification and a fresh verification are
different observations. Neither UI grants order authority.

Each PC retains its own research membership. Combined planning uses one shared
cash balance with explicit holdings, reservations and source ownership. Planned
sales are conditional projections; live buying power comes from usable broker
cash and confirmed execution evidence. Planning price/cash ranges and assumed fills confer no order authority.
Accepted-plan quantities bound due intentions; actual cash, inventory, quotes
and reservations can reduce executable quantities.

Atlas is the designated sole execution host in the reviewed combined design;
Scout is a forecast/research producer and synthesis owner. Actual enforcement
requires local role, cutover, ownership and source evidence. No peer notice or
missing counterpart transfers that authority. The human's manual start/stop
controls remain decisive. The current native Trader Representative includes
conditional guarded-start/repair authority, while the reviewed installation
evidence identifies staged launcher dependencies. Older manual-only runbook
wording and that newer prompt differ. Neither a task definition, guarded-start
proposal nor registration proves an installed compatible launcher or worker.

The stock horizons remain independent in forecast identity and owned inventory.
The earlier hourly entry with four-hour confirmation/veto describes legacy
behavior. Policies must be identified explicitly: the manual current-market
policy, historical fixed-budget policy, own-horizon selling and the approved
capped fallback are not interchangeable. Current cumulative catch-up reconciles
due quantities against fills, open orders and reservations; it does not blindly
replay missed timestamp actions. See [independent horizons](INDEPENDENT_STOCK_HORIZONS.md).

Named Loop C retains its independently established options research proposal
sizing, observer, risk and evidence scope. Its read-only observer is distinct from the operational stock
adapter and its historical weekly review. The pooled sequence encoder remains
a separate feature/candidate path. A pause or missing local receipt does not
retire any of those shared contracts. Options intent remains paper/no-trade;
future option execution requires separately authorized same-leg revalidation.

## Recovery and evidence limits

Use the installed responsibility/runbook procedure and exact local binding for
real recovery. Inspect the original failed stage, source/configuration hashes,
lease/owner identity and dated receipt. Do not rerun completed stages, clear
locks, switch to a previous pointer, restart legacy supervisors or enable a trader
because a documentation example exists. The current recovery implementation can
perform bounded reviewed repairs under its own authority; old prose saying
there is never any automatic retry is not a current universal restriction.

Historical numerical tests, saved receipts, source installation, task configuration,
ordinary runtime observations and completed outcomes remain separately labeled.
This audit ran document checks only. It contacted no provider or broker, trained
no model, replayed no workflow and changed no task, binding, control or worker.
