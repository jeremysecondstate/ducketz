## Completion and recovery

Record the actual persisted data results and unresolved gaps separately from
completed-session review, model decisions and installation. A launched process,
saved proposal or installed specification does not establish live trader
readiness. There is no combined Gameplan or delivery stage after preparation.

Finish eligible owned 15-minute work under existing standing authority. Preserve
completed stages, per-symbol checkpoints, original incident identities and actual
errors after interruptions. A late wake, missed window, unavailable provider or
chat/model interruption is not cancellation. Continue independent work and resume
eligible unfinished work; do not retune against a frozen holdout, substitute an
older session or invent missing observations to claim completion.

Initial trader activation and stop remain Jeremy's manual or existing-task
actions. Preparation and maintenance do not start, stop or restart a trader or
data service, clear a manual stop, submit orders or extend a logical run. Scout's
already-authorized application-managed replacements remain governed by
[the iteration lifecycle](scout-iteration-restarts.md).

Review and publish completed shared repairs through the verified installed
coordination procedures, preserving other writers and successful publication
stages. Publication does not gate authorized preparation. Keep credentials, raw
data, fitted models, private recipes, account state, native bindings and task
memory local. Report meaningful results, failures and next resumable actions;
avoid unchanged acknowledgments.
