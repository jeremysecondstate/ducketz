# Independent 15-minute model ownership

Jeremy's October 16, 2026 instruction applies in both directions: Scout and
Atlas select and assess their own models using their own symbols and data.
A peer's successful model is evidence to investigate, not a recipe to install.
This narrows the shared-strategy-default rule for 15-minute model tuning; it
does not split the common correctness, provenance, UI or execution engine.

## Scope of adoption

Shared source may fix leakage, timestamp handling, causal calibration, missing
data accounting, scope validation or display errors. Adoption must preserve
the local watchlist, model specifications, calibration choices, history limits,
feature/data-population contract and frozen session decisions. A source change
that alters any of these model choices needs a separate local assessment. It
must not silently redefine a private recipe through a changed shared default.

Private manual recipes bind the exact PC and symbol set. Version 2 recipes
store resolved estimator, preprocessing and calibration parameters, dependency
compatibility and a versioned ordered feature contract. They consume those saved
values instead of the current shared registry defaults. Migrating a legacy
recipe is an explicit local operation: validate its ownership and checksum,
preserve the original, verify equivalent effective settings, and record the new
identity. Loading a recipe never rewrites it. Incompatible contracts fail
explicitly. Existing frozen cycles and historical policies retain their evidence.

New assessments freeze resolved model contracts before validation and carry
those exact contracts into qualified supervised policy installations. Legacy
recipes and policies remain readable with their original default inheritance;
upgrading shared source alone does not freeze or migrate them. An old pending
decision cannot claim that today's defaults were its evaluated frozen settings.
A supervised policy and an explicitly configured manual
experiment are different selections. The current manual recipe overrides
supervised-policy adoption; a successful nightly policy assessment does not
qualify that separate manual experiment.

## Sparse observations and qualification

Use actual source coverage, observed training support, class balance, price
staleness, elapsed gaps and chronological performance to diagnose a symbol.
Market capitalization alone neither establishes overfitting nor chooses a model.
EQUS.MINI is a particular feed; source-empty minutes are not proof that nobody
traded the stock anywhere. Preserve missing observations and source identity.
Never create market-wide no-trade evidence from an absent provider row.

The observed-only recipe excludes synthetic rows before deriving features and
labels, retains elapsed gaps, and requires the exact eligible observed reference.
It does not replace an ineligible reference with a nearby candle. Keep minimum
rows, dates, classes and calibration support; insufficient symbols remain
unavailable. More regularization does not manufacture missing evidence.

Compare probabilities with actual observed outcomes, proper probability scores
and simple baselines. A 96% forecast is not 96% measured accuracy. Separate
synthetic outcomes from genuinely missing outcomes and never count synthetic
labels as observed validation. Report sample size and class prevalence beside
accuracy; the observed majority-class accuracy is descriptive hindsight, not a
deployable strategy chosen without future knowledge.

Preserve chronological training/calibration/validation separation, purged
overlapping targets, and the most recent completed session as the held-out gate.
Later wakes may refresh data and results, but cannot retune against that frozen
session, replace it with an older day, or lower gates to declare readiness.

## Operating and publication boundaries

Recipes, raw data, fitted models, account state and ledgers stay private.
Publish reviewed common source and offline tests through the pinned completion
queue. An exact source installation and a process's loaded implementation are
separate facts. Historical forecasts keep their original settings and timestamps.
Refreshing their display does not refit them or establish present readiness.

Prepare the local manual launcher and prerequisites without starting, restarting
or enabling a trader, clearing a manual stop, or placing orders. Current live
provider and broker readiness are separate from completed-session assessment.
