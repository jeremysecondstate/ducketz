production, Gameplan Stats, model review, synthesis and delivery. Current trading
and preparation use only the independent 15-minute systems on Atlas and Scout.
This supersedes earlier instructions to produce or recover a combined Gameplan.
Retired tasks remain paused or disabled; historical memory and missing obsolete
outputs do not authorize their restoration.

Each PC's 15-minute trader makes its own forecasts, uses its own existing broker
connection and actual capacity, manages its own order identities and fills, and
keeps its own operating state and logs. Another trader's process, private ledger,
readiness, source version, lock or approval is not a prerequisite. Shared source
and broker connectivity do not create a shared execution ledger or a runtime
dependency. Retain existing order identities, reservations, fill attribution and
known-order recovery without inventing fills or rewriting history.

Each PC keeps its own symbols, model selection, hyperparameters, calibration,
fitted models and data-population recipe. Shared correctness repairs preserve
those local choices under [independent model ownership](intraday-model-ownership.md).
A qualified supervised policy does not qualify a separate manual recipe.
