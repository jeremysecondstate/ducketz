October 1 preparation: quality fields and bounded diagnosis order

This is a source review and diagnosis plan, not a finding that any October 1
model has passed or failed. No fitting, source changes, source-archive scans or
final audit were performed to prepare it.

1. Keep the three evidence layers distinct.

Loop B (ml.prediction_runtime / ml.runtime_pipeline):
  The materialization log's [rolling horizon] symbol READY concerns route input
  availability. Its final result is COMPLETED or COMPLETED_WITH_LIMITATIONS
  from route_errors. This scheduled invocation requires all routes; unresolved
  required route/model errors block atomic publication. Its manifest binds
  configuration.models, model_feature_sets, partition_configuration_by_horizon,
  route_errors, pricing_evidence and publication_counts. Reused models and
  retained frozen weekly rows must retain their original evidence.
  Loop B model manifests under ml/models/<horizon>/<model>/<generation> record
  offline_evaluation.status=OFFLINE_EVALUATED_CANDIDATE, raw_model,
  calibrated_model, training_base_rate, calibration_base_rate,
  calibration_support, assessment_used_for_training/calibration=false and
  lockbox.status=CLOSED_UNTOUCHED_UNSCORED. These are not the independent
  Gameplan's four promotion gates and are not sizing qualification.

Independent directional Gameplan (model-reports.json, keyed 1h/4h/1d/1w):
  promotion_gate.status is PROMOTED or RESEARCH_NOT_PROMOTED.
  promotion_gate.policy_version must match the saved version; new independent
  runs use independent-stock-directional-promotion-v2.
  promotion_gate.checks lists each failed/satisfied condition:
    calibration_retains_directional_information
    assessment_has_at_least_10_decision_clusters
    expected_calibration_error_at_most_0_15
    brier_within_baseline_tolerance
    log_loss_within_baseline_tolerance
  Numeric fields: assessment, assessment_raw_scores,
  training_base_rate_assessment, calibration_raw_scores,
  calibration_assessment_change, calibration_diagnostics,
  promotion_gate.baseline_tolerances, assessment_minus_baseline and
  assessment_beats_baseline. New Brier <= baseline+0.005 and logloss <=
  baseline+0.01 do not establish baseline outperformance.
  Calibration must retain >1e-12 variation in calibration and assessment,
  information_available=true, nondecreasing_constraint_active=false and a
  calibration positive rate strictly between zero and one.
  Partition/support fields: partitions, partition_decision_clusters,
  target_support_by_symbol, assessment_by_route/symbol, target_boundary_quality,
  features.admitted/count. Per forecast row, model_status additionally becomes
  RESEARCH_NO_TARGET_HISTORY if exact symbol or symbol-route fitted rows are zero.
  Save actual symbol_fitted_target_rows and symbol_route_fitted_target_rows;
  a pooled group pass is not proof of every route's fitted support.
  Development fields: selected_family, selection_metrics,
  logistic_regularization_candidates/selected_logistic_regularization_c,
  calibration_selection, development_selection_policy; weekly additionally
  records selected_base_family, selected_probability_shrinkage_weight,
  probability_shrinkage_weights, selection_shrinkage_prior/fitted_shrinkage_prior.
  These use the fixed candidate grids and development partitions only.

  Important: a quality fail produces FIT_WARNING in gameplan/<horizon>/assessment
  or /calibration but can still produce a successful native publication with
  an honest research status. A COMPLETE process or clean manifest is not proof
  all models passed. The prepared final verifier records research as coverage
  notes; it does not replace the user's requested defect investigation.

  Champion caveat: model-reports[group].deployment can state
  RETAINED_VERIFIED_CHAMPION, source_run, retained_cohort_output,
  challenger_cohort_output, challenger_report_output and challenger_status.
  Always review the saved fresh challenger report as well as the published model.
  A retained model pass must not hide a fresh failure. Native retention is only
  the latest compatible promoted source for the SAME action date, universe,
  probability target, target price source and feature contract; it does not
  select among models by observed assessment score.

Independent learned sizing (stock-trader-model-runs/<run>/training-report.json):
  Top-level MODELS_FIT and horizons[group].status=FITTED mean fitting happened.
  They do not imply qualified execution scope. Distinguish model.json
  horizons[group].fitted and training-report.horizons[group].fitted_scope_count
  from qualified_scope_count, scope_readiness[scope].status/reason,
  supported_horizons and qualified_target_contracts.
  Current pooled-horizon-with-observed-target-coverage-v1 requires a passing
  untouched horizon assessment (>=10 decision clusters), at least one fitted
  exact symbol/route/duration cluster and >=20 fitted pooled symbol/route
  clusters across observed durations. Diagnostic-only scopes remain RESEARCH.
  Quality is stricter than directional-v2: Brier and logloss strictly beat their
  training baselines, ECE<=0.15, return_mse strictly below baseline,
  adverse_mse<=baseline and probability range >1e-8.
  scope_readiness reason distinguishes UNSEEN_EXACT_TARGET_SCOPE,
  INSUFFICIENT_POOLED_SYMBOL_ROUTE_EVIDENCE,
  HELD_OUT_HORIZON_QUALITY_NOT_PROMOTED and
  HELD_OUT_POOLED_HORIZON_QUALIFIED_WITH_OBSERVED_SCOPE.
  Report partition_evidence, head_training_objectives,
  head_development_selection, calibration_selection and market_feature_admission.
  The archive admission policy excludes only execution rows whose eight optional
  market features are all absent; partial/nonfinite observations still fail.
  Read-only loaders reproduce this admission and scope quality from the bound
  immutable Gameplan cohorts. Research sizing is not relabeled, and it does not
  become a separate veto for the user-selected manual direction policy.

2. If a new failure appears, capture before attempting a repair.

  While training is active, preserve exact FIT_WARNING/FIT_ERROR JSON and its
  group/phase, child/native run identity, source/deadline, new log offsets and
  stage heartbeat. Target-quality excluded_rows is not alone a model failure;
  distinguish it from final calibration/assessment failed_checks.
  Once a native group report is durably saved, read its small JSON diagnostics.
  Do not start a duplicate fit or reconstruct archives concurrently merely to
  learn progress. The root runs full immutable verification after COMPLETE.
  Retain original reports and model/cohort hashes before any justified fix.

3. Diagnose a defect rather than optimize the final assessment.

  First reproduce numeric gate arithmetic and saved source/target identity.
  Check data clocks, raw-return label arithmetic, exact observed endpoints within
  five minutes, duplicate natural targets, causal source cutoffs, class support,
  purged chronological partitions and all expected symbols/routes.
  Inspect archive-history.json exclusions, warmup/split/provider-quality resets,
  source dates and cohort ranges. Observed sparse endpoints or excluded invalid
  bars are evidence limits, not permission to fabricate rows or widen tolerance.
  Check finite admitted features and fitting/convergence diagnostics. Candidate
  comparison remains on fixed development data/grid. Check selected_family is
  the development minimum under the documented tie rule; verify calibration
  selection/refit eligibility and assessment_used_for_selection=false. For weekly
  models verify shrinkage priors, fixed weights and preserved variation.
  Use saved fitted artifacts to reproduce assessment scores once, after COMPLETE;
  do not rank/reselect alternatives on that assessment. The prepared final audit
  does this and independently reproduces archive cohorts/current inference.
  A data/implementation/convergence defect supports a concrete tested repair.
  A faithfully reproduced quality shortfall alone does not justify threshold
  relaxation, invented labels, a new grid chosen from assessment results, a
  repeated unchanged fit, or a promised pass.

4. Completion and repair authority remain separate.

  Investigate promptly even if native stage status succeeds. If a concrete
  repair is supported, root must use the documented owned controlled-stop/
  recovery and eligible failed-stage resume mechanism, preserving successful
  upstream work and the original October 1 11:00Z deadline. Do not manufacture
  a failed receipt, edit an immutable publication or blindly rerun a completed
  source session. A newly discovered repair never permits repeating a source
  session that is already fully COMPLETE. No changes are proposed by this guide.
  This review grants no trader start, order, controls, strategy or rollout change.
