## How quantities and prices are planned

Each projected size starts with `account value × min(0.15 × horizon weight / 10, 0.05)`, using weights 1, 2, 3 and 4 for hourly, four-hour, daily and weekly opportunities. Current cash, total exposure and the stock's existing investment can reduce that capacity. Whole-share quantities use the upper end of the planning price range.

Scheduled entries also apply the forecast-confidence rule and share one cash budget, capped at 95.00% of available cash, 130.00% gross account exposure and 15.00% per-stock exposure. The minimum order is $25.00, with at most 6 entries in a batch. Expected future sale proceeds are excluded.

Planning prices use the middle 90% of observed historical moves from the prior session's close to the entry time. An actual entry rechecks the ask LIMIT price, cash, holdings and trading controls at its scheduled time.
